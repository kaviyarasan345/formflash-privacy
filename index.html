FormFlash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FormFlash ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the FormFlash mobile application ("the App").

1. Information and data handled by the App

Phone number and authentication

FormFlash uses Firebase Phone Authentication to verify your phone number and sign you in. Your phone number and authentication-related information may be processed by Google Firebase to provide this service and help prevent abuse.

Documents, scans and PDFs

FormFlash provides document scanning and PDF-related features. Documents may be stored locally on your device and may also be stored in cloud storage, depending on the feature you use. Files kept only on your device may remain there until you remove them or uninstall the App, subject to your device's behavior and settings.

Before using any feature that uploads or syncs a document, review the relevant in-app information. Do not upload documents containing sensitive information unless you intend to use that feature.

Advertising and device information

FormFlash uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial and native advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as IP address, advertising or other device identifiers, ad interactions, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google's handling of this information depends on the SDK version, configuration and applicable settings.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure .

2. How information is used

Information may be processed to:

  • Verify your phone number and provide account access.
  • Provide document scanning and PDF features you choose to use.
  • Store or retrieve documents when a feature uses cloud storage.
  • Display advertisements and help prevent advertising fraud.
  • Maintain, protect and troubleshoot the App and its services.

3. Service providers and sharing

We use Google Firebase for phone authentication and Google AdMob for advertising. These services may process information as needed to provide their respective services, subject to their terms and privacy practices.

Document data may be processed by a cloud storage provider when a feature uploads or syncs files. The specific provider and the data it receives must be confirmed from the App's implementation before this policy is published.

We do not describe other sharing practices here because they must be confirmed from the App's actual implementation and service configuration.

4. Storage and security

Some files may be stored on your device, while other files may be stored in cloud services when the relevant feature is used. Security depends on the device, app implementation and services involved. We recommend keeping your device secure and using its screen lock.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Retention and account deletion

You can request deletion of your FormFlash account by using the account-deletion option provided in the App, if available, or by contacting us at formflash.official@gmail.com .

When an account deletion request is received, we will process it in accordance with applicable requirements and delete associated account data that we control, subject to any legal obligations and any limited retention that is actually required. Files stored only on your device may need to be deleted separately from the device.

The exact deletion process, cloud-file deletion behavior, backup retention and any legally required retention periods must be confirmed before publication. Do not assume that deleting the account automatically deletes every local file or cloud backup unless the implementation does so.

6. Your choices

You can choose which documents to scan or process and can manage files stored on your device through the App or your device's file controls. You may also manage advertising-related settings through your Android device, where available.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not represented by this policy as being designed specifically for children. If the App is intended for children or targets children, its audience settings, advertising configuration and applicable privacy requirements must be reviewed separately.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated revision date.
